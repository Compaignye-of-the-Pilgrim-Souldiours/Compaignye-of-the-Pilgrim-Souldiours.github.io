Join the Compaignye

Beginners are welcome. New members serve a probationary period for kit, persona, and safety before full voting rights as Reenactor Members.

What to expect

You will build toward documented 1375–1425 kit (linen and wool, historical construction), keep a Kit History file, and — if martial — train under the Yeoman to ALHF/QLHF standards.

Associate membership is available for non-martial support roles (logistics, photography, research).
