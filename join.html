Join the Compaignye

We welcome people who want to research, make, and present late-medieval life with care — including absolute beginners.

What to expect

Membership status, insurance renewals, and marshal certifications will be tracked in an association-owned membership sheet (not a public form on this site). Enquiries go to role mailboxes so continuity does not depend on a personal Gmail account.
