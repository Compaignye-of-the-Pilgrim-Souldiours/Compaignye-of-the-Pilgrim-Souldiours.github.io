Next training session

Semester 1, Week 1 (Curriculum V2) — Armored posture, stance mechanics, traditional two-handed guards, and the half-sword transition. Fiore dei Liberi, Il Fior di Battaglia (c. 1409): De Spada a doi Mani & De Spada in Arme. 90 minutes at rotating Maryborough / Hervey Bay private grounds.

Week 1 lesson plan (V2) · Harnischfechten curriculum (V2) · Videos by Federico Malagutti

Safety. Pure weapons only — zero thrust, zero grappling. ALHF / QLHF guidelines. Drive cuts with the hips to spare the shoulders under spaulders.

Session objectives

  1. Complete mutual squiring, strapping, and ALHF/QLHF armoured safety inspection.
  2. Form and balance Posta di Donna in Arme and Tutta Porta di Ferro in harness, allowing for spaulder articulation and helmet ocularia.
  3. Execute traditional two-handed mandritti fendenti driven by hip rotation without harness impingement.
  4. Transition smoothly from a two-handed long-measure grip into Posta di Vera Croce (half-sword).
  5. Run controlled partner distance and displacement checks under the no-thrust / no-grappling protocol.

  • 0:20

Guards & actions this week

  • Posta di Donna in Arme

    Modified lady’s guard: right foot back; hilt near the shoulder with elbows bent under the spaulders; blade angled back. Primary chamber for heavy mandritti fendenti — do not lock the arms overhead.

  • Tutta Porta di Ferro

    Low long-measure rampart: lead foot forward; hands low before the fauld; blade angled down. Recovery seat after a descending cut; shields the legs.

  • Dritto Finestra

    Window guard at chest-to-neck height for partner intercepts: step slightly off-line with volta stabile and meet the descending blade on the strong.

  • Posta di Vera Croce

    Half-sword True Cross: dominant hand on the hilt, off-hand on the mid-blade across the breastplate. Absorbs a cut and levers the opposing blade offline — no forward thrust after the displacement.
