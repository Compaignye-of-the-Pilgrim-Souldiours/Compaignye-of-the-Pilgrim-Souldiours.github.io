Public summary

Safety. Martial activity runs under marshal oversight. Weapons and armour are inspected and logged. Anyone may call “HOLD.”

Membership. Probationary period before full voting rights; ALHF insurance/safety compliance; QLHF standards as a goal.

Digital assets. Domain, email, cloud storage, and this website belong to the association — not to individual administrators.
