Policies

Short public stubs. Full procedures live with the committee; operational templates are in the association’s repository and Workspace.

Safety

Martial activity runs under marshal oversight. Weapons and armour are inspected and logged. Anyone may stop an activity for a safety concern. Incident templates and insurance certificates are stored in Operational Records (Drive), not in this git repository.

Membership

Membership status, renewals, and certifications are tracked in a protected membership sheet. Sensitive health or insurance detail is limited to the Prior and Armorum. Public pages never publish personal member data.

Digital asset ownership

Domain registrations, role email, cloud storage, social accounts, and this website belong to the association. Individuals who create or administer them do so as custodians. Committee minutes should record that principle — use the wording in the repository’s docs/governance/DIGITAL-ASSET-OWNERSHIP.md.

Contact the Prior for policy questions.
