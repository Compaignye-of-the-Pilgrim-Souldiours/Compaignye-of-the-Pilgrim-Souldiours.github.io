About the Compaignye

Societas Militum Peregrinorum — motto Peregrini in Via, Fratres in Armis. Inspired by Chaucer’s diverse fellowship, we portray a functional company of English travellers, 1375–1425.

Period & purpose

Our objects concentrate on the life, material culture, and martial arts of late medieval England between 1375 and 1425, through living history and historical combat interpretation.

Documentary evidence — manuscripts, effigies, archaeological finds — is required to justify kit. We aim to move from “costume” to historical clothing under ALHF and QLHF standards.

Arms

Or, a falcon displayed proper, wearing about its neck a collar Azure secured with three bezants, bearing in its dexter claw a bourdon terminating in a cross potent, and in its sinister claw an escallop shell Argent.

Chapter officers

Constitutional titles follow Chaucer’s road; legal roles are President, Secretary, Marshal, and Treasurer.

  • Host (President) Public head of the association; sets company tone and represents the Compaignye outward.
  • Clerk (Secretary) Correspondence, rolls, and the register of members.
  • Yeoman (Marshal) Arms, armour inspections, field safety, and Harnischfechten standards.
  • Franklin (Treasurer) Ledger, permitted group assets, and (with the Host) sensitive membership records.

Membership classes

  • Reenactor Members Full voting rights; martial and living-history participation to kit and safety standards.
  • Probationary Members Training, kit review, and documentation phase; no voting rights until completed.
  • Associate Members Non-martial support (logistics, photography, research assistance); no voting rights.
