About the Compaignye

We are building a focused late-medieval fellowship: soldiers and pilgrims of the generations around the turn of the fifteenth century.

Period window

Our working frame is roughly 1375–1425. That span covers the later Hundred Years’ War, shifting armour fashion, and a rich manuscript culture we can cite in public education.

Kit choices, camp interpretation, and fighting presentation are expected to sit inside that window unless a specific educational contrast is labelled as such.

Roles (working titles)

  • Prior Head of the association; overall governance and sensitive records access with the Armorum.
  • Armorum Arms, armour, inspections, and martial safety oversight.
  • Arca Treasurer; digital platform continuity, Workspace billing, and shared storage stewardship.

Why a digital footprint matters

Beyond the legal minimum, we treat our digital tools as corporate memory: research library, marshal ledgers, insurance certificates, and role email that survive committee rotation.

Public channels stay educational and promotional. Private member discussion lives in a dedicated member hub. Operational records live in association-owned Google Workspace once that is established — see the repository’s DIGITAL-PLATFORM.md.
