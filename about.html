About the Compaignye

Societas Militum Peregrinorum — motto Peregrini in Via, Fratres in Armis (“Pilgrims on the Road, Brothers in Arms”). We research, reconstruct, and teach the life and martial culture of the medieval traveller and soldier.

Period window

Association objects and combat by-laws focus on late medieval England, 1375–1425. Kit and fighting presentation are expected to sit inside that core window unless a specific educational contrast is labelled as such.

The Charter’s Code of Conduct also speaks to documentation for gear across a wider medieval span; any broader interpretation will be settled when foundational documents are adopted.

Chapter officers

  • Prior Head of the association; governance and (with the Arca) sensitive membership records.
  • Armorum Marshal — arms, armour, inspections, and martial safety oversight.
  • Arca Treasurer; Workspace stewardship and (with the Prior) sensitive membership records.

Why a digital footprint matters

Beyond the legal minimum, we treat our digital tools as corporate memory: research library, marshal ledgers, insurance certificates, and role email that survive committee rotation.

Public channels stay educational and promotional. Private member discussion lives in a dedicated member hub. Operational records live in association-owned Google Workspace once that is established — see the repository’s DIGITAL-PLATFORM.md.
