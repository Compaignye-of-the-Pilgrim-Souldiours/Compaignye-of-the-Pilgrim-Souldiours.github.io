# Digital asset ownership

## Principle

All digital assets created or used for The Compaignye of the Pilgrim Souldiours belong to the **association**, not to the individuals who registered, designed, or administer them.

This includes (without limitation): domain names, role email accounts, Google Workspace tenancy, cloud storage, membership spreadsheets, operational forms and their responses, social media accounts, Discord (or other) member hubs, and the public website / GitHub organization.

Office-holders act as **custodians**. On leaving office they must hand over credentials, recovery codes, and admin rights to the incoming officers or to the Clerk/Host.

## Suggested committee minute wording

Copy or adapt the following into the next committee meeting minutes:

> **Digital assets.** The committee confirms that all digital assets of the association — including domain names, email accounts, cloud storage, membership and operational records, social media accounts, member communication platforms, and the public website and related code repositories — are the property of the association. Members who create or administer such assets do so as custodians on behalf of the association and must transfer administration and recovery materials on leaving office. The Franklin and Host shall maintain an access register (without storing passwords in shared minutes).

## What not to put in minutes or git

- Passwords, 2FA secrets, backup codes, API tokens
- Full membership PII dumps

Record **who holds** recovery materials (e.g. “Host sealed envelope / Franklin password manager org vault”) without writing the secrets themselves.
